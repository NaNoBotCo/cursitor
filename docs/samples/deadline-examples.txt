DEADLINE EXAMPLES

Each date is printed with the arithmetic that produced it. Generated by `cursitor deadline from`; the command is on the line under each heading.

1. California discovery responses, served by email over Labor Day
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-08-05 --method email --rule ca.discovery_response
Due Wed Sep 9 2026 — served by email Aug 5 + 30 days (CCP §2030.260) = Fri Sep 4; + 2 court days e-service (§1010.6(a)(3)(B)), skipping Mon Sep 7 Labor Day = Wed Sep 9.

2. California requests for admission, served by mail inside California (the example matter's next deadline)
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-09-04 --method mail --rule ca.rfa_response
Due Fri Oct 9 2026 — served by mail Sep 4 + 30 days (CCP §2033.250(a)) = Sun Oct 4; + 5 days mail within California (§1013(a)) = Fri Oct 9.

3. California motion to compel further responses: 45 days from verified responses served by email
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-09-09 --method email --rule ca.motion_compel_further
Due Tue Oct 27 2026 — served by email Sep 9 + 45 days (CCP §2030.300(c)) = Sat Oct 24; + 2 court days e-service (§1010.6(a)(3)(B)) = Tue Oct 27.
  Note: The 45 days run from the verified response; an unverified response, or a later written extension agreed by the parties, changes the trigger. A deadline agreed by letter is MOVED only when the confirming writing is on disk.

4. California §12a roll: served by hand, the 30th day falls on a Saturday before Labor Day
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-08-06 --method personal --rule ca.rog_response
Due Tue Sep 8 2026 — served by personal delivery Aug 6 + 30 days (CCP §2030.260(a)) = Sat Sep 5; Sat Sep 5 is a Saturday, and Mon Sep 7 is Labor Day; rolls to Tue Sep 8 (CCP §12a).

5. Federal requests for production: Rule 6(d) adds 3 days for mail, none for electronic service
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-08-05 --method mail --rule fed.rfp_response
Due Tue Sep 8 2026 — served by mail Aug 5 + 30 days (FRCP 34(b)(2)(A)) = Fri Sep 4; + 3 days mail (FRCP 6(d)) = Mon Sep 7; Mon Sep 7 is Labor Day, a court holiday; rolls to Tue Sep 8 (FRCP 6(a)(1)(C)).
$ cursitor deadline from --served 2026-08-05 --method email --rule fed.rfp_response
Due Fri Sep 4 2026 — served by electronic means Aug 5 + 30 days (FRCP 34(b)(2)(A)) = Fri Sep 4; no added days for electronic service (FRCP 6(d) leaves out Rule 5(b)(2)(E) since Dec. 1, 2016).
